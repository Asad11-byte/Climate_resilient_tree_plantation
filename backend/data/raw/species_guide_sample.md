# Tree Species for Mandi Bahauddin

## Shisham (Dalbergia sissoo)

### Soil Requirements

Shisham grows best in well-drained alluvial soils with neutral to slightly
alkaline pH, typical of the Chenab riverine belt near Mandi Bahauddin. It
performs poorly in waterlogged or heavily saline soils.

### Water and Climate Tolerance

Shisham has moderate drought tolerance once established but requires
consistent moisture during its first two growing seasons. It tolerates the
hot summers typical of central Punjab but is sensitive to prolonged
waterlogging after monsoon rains.

## Kikar (Acacia nilotica)

### Soil Requirements

Kikar tolerates a wide range of soil types, including saline and
waterlogged soils, making it suitable for marginal agricultural land around
Mandi Bahauddin.

### Water and Climate Tolerance

Kikar is highly drought tolerant and heat tolerant, making it a resilient
choice under increasing summer temperatures linked to climate change in
Punjab.

## Plantation Guidance

### Planting Season

Both Shisham and Kikar are typically planted at the start of the monsoon
season (July) to take advantage of natural rainfall during establishment.
Nursery-raised saplings are recommended over direct seeding for higher
survival rates in the Mandi Bahauddin district.

### Spacing

Recommended spacing for agroforestry plantation is 3x3 meters for Kikar and
4x4 meters for Shisham, based on Punjab Forest Department nursery guidance.

## Climate Change Adaptation

Rising average temperatures and shifting rainfall patterns in Punjab are
increasing interest in drought- and heat-tolerant species such as Kikar for
climate-resilient agroforestry programs in districts including Mandi
Bahauddin.
